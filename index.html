Suggested teaching actions

Generated locally from demo metrics
1. Increase active participationUse a one-minute think–pair–share activity, then invite responses from different parts of the room.
2. Support learners who missed a sessionShare a concise recap and a short formative quiz so absent learners can identify gaps.
3. Check understanding before moving onRun a two-question exit ticket. Use the response distribution to decide whether to revisit the topic.
4. Protect privacyKeep attendance records access-controlled, define a retention period, and provide a correction process for mistaken records.
These are transparent sample recommendations, not outputs from a connected LLM. A production AI agent can use a locally hosted model or a consented API, with human review.
